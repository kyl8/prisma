# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated from the brief: React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, and lightweight headless UI patterns where interaction requires them. Frontend-only navigable mockup with no backend or production integrations.

## Users

Primary users are customs brokers and small-to-medium importers preparing maritime import operations before cargo arrival. They need to exchange, complete, review, and correct product-catalog and shipment information without fragmented messages or repeated rework. Terminal operations teams are an important secondary audience because earlier readiness signals improve resource planning.

## Product Purpose

PRISMA centralizes product catalogs, documents, and pre-arrival coordination in a shared workspace. It helps the importer complete information, helps the customs broker review what must be corrected, and makes the operation's readiness visible before omissions become delays and costs.

Success for this mockup means that a first-time visitor can understand the problem, see how the shared workflow works, explore the dashboard and assistant, and reach a clear request-demo action without assuming that any live integration already exists.

## Positioning

Instead of showing only a DUIMP status or isolated documents, PRISMA combines cadastral, documentary, financial, and operational signals into an early, actionable view of import readiness.

## Operating Context

The product is used before cargo arrival, around the Product Catalog, NCM classification, required product attributes, shipment documents, review comments, and coordination between importer and customs broker. The proposed assistant is linked conceptually to Logcomex and helps users understand and complete the workflow.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Shared client workspace created from a CNPJ.
- Product catalog organized per client.
- NCM-informed guidance for required attributes.
- Importer data completion and customs-broker review/correction workflow.
- Readiness dashboard for cadastral, documentary, financial, and operational signals.
- Conversational assistant mockup for platform guidance.
- No backend, authentication, live DUIMP, Logcomex, Siscomex, or terminal integration in this deliverable.
- All operational values and company names shown in the interface are illustrative.
- Portuguese (Brazil) is the primary language.

## Brand Commitments

The confirmed product name is PRISMA. The homepage must be monochromatic and restrained, with the editorial clarity, large-scale typography, spacious alternating product demonstrations, and subtle motion found in Kontu's marketing site. Streamline-style consistent line icons and selective Skiper UI-inspired motion patterns may support the experience. The design must remain original to PRISMA and its port-logistics subject.

## Product Principles

1. Surface readiness early enough to act, not merely to report.
2. Turn missing information into specific next actions.
3. Keep importer and customs broker in one shared source of truth.
4. Be explicit about evidence limits and illustrative mock data.
5. Reduce operational uncertainty without replacing specialist judgment.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for contrast, keyboard operation, focus visibility, semantic structure, reduced-motion preferences, and readable Portuguese copy. The responsive experience must remain complete on mobile rather than becoming a reduced desktop page.
