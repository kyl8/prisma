# PRISMA Homepage Direction Contract

## Mode

Persuade: a first-time customs broker, importer, or terminal stakeholder should understand the value, see the workflow, and choose to explore or discuss the prototype.

## World

Monochromatic operational editorialism. Warm white, graphite, and restrained gray create the calm clarity of a working document and a modern logistics workspace. The visual pacing follows the supplied Kontu reference: oversized plain-language typography, generous silence, alternating product demonstrations, and a dark grounded close. PRISMA remains original through its import-readiness content, shared-review mechanics, and product-specific mock data.

## First Viewport

The headline states the consequence and value: "Veja o risco antes da carga chegar." The supporting copy names the mechanism. A realistic readiness dashboard occupies the lower half of the viewport and proves the product immediately with cadastral, documentary, financial, and operational signals. Illustrative data is labeled.

## Visitor Path

Problem consequence -> shared product catalog -> NCM-guided attributes -> importer/broker correction loop -> contextual assistant -> complete readiness dashboard -> evidence-aware FAQ -> conversation CTA.

## Signature Interaction

The same operational context changes under the visitor's hand: dashboard tabs reveal pending actions, catalog filtering isolates products requiring action, NCM requirements can be completed, and the assistant answers three contextual prompts. The interactions are local frontend simulations and never imply a live integration.

## Motion Grammar

One restrained material: content settles vertically with exponential ease, the hero window is revealed through a clipped frame, progress responds once, and state changes crossfade. Content remains readable before motion completes and `prefers-reduced-motion` removes nonessential transitions.

## Responsive Contract

Desktop uses editorial pairs with product and explanation side by side. Mobile becomes a complete single-column narrative, preserves every product demonstration and interaction, hides only the app sidebar chrome, and has no horizontal overflow.

## Quality Bar

The supplied Kontu screenshots and current Kontu homepage are the structural craft reference, not a pixel-copy target. The intended bar is quiet hierarchy, precise product UI, generous spacing, and a coherent monochrome system across marketing and mock application surfaces.

## Honest Risk

Minimal monochrome marketing can become generic. PRISMA avoids that risk by showing specific logistics terminology, evidence-aware copy, the shared correction workflow, and complete functional product simulations rather than decorative cards or invented claims.
